Our Ethos

Designed for Life. Crafted with Ethics.

At AURA Atelier, we reject disposable fashion. Our mission is to produce timeless, accessible, high-performance garments using certified organic and recycled materials while maintaining 100% fair-wage artisan labor.

100% Traceable Supply Chain

Every sweater, coat, and accessory in our catalog is traced from seed to stitch. We collaborate exclusively with certified European and Asian ateliers operating on renewable solar power and zero-waste water recycling systems.
